Two-factor authentication, often shortened to 2FA, adds a second proof of identity when you sign in. After your password, you also provide something only you have, such as a code from an authenticator app. It matters because a stolen password alone is then not enough to get into your account.

In this demo app the two-factor setup control is under Settings, in the Advanced area. It is a demo placeholder: pressing it does not change anything about your account.
