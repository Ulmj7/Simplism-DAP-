tags: privacy, private, password, email, hidden

---
The help assistant never sees what you type into password fields, and it does not see the contents of private fields such as your email address. Some parts of a page are marked to be ignored by the help layer completely, and it treats those parts as if they were not there.

The assistant works from a list of page elements that the app has registered, with their names and descriptions. It does not read the values of your fields.
