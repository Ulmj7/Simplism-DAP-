tags: help, assistant, guide, walkthrough, limits

visibility: public
---
The help assistant can answer questions about this app using approved help articles, and it can suggest a guided walkthrough that shows you where to click. A walkthrough only highlights things on the page and waits for you. You always do the actions yourself.

The assistant cannot press buttons for you, change your settings, read your private fields, browse the web, or answer questions that are not about this app.
